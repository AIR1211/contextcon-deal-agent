Deep run used 73.5 credits. It was stopped early, so the checks listed under NOT TESTED did not finish.
Small samples: treat percentages as indicative only. Access differs per account: GET /account/endpoints.

COMPANY IDENTITY (free)
Real brand domains: 16/16 correct.
Lookalike or fake domains: 5/5 returned no match. No match means "can't verify", not proof of fraud.
Names: top result was the expected brand in 10/17. Ties at the top score are common (up to 25).
Legal or parent names (Imagine Marketing, Honasa, FSN) returned separate records with no link to the brand.
Rule: identify by domain. Keep our own alias table with sources.
