DEMO PLAN
Goal: show one full deal, then show it surviving failure. Runs the same whether live or recorded.

SAY THIS EARLY, AND KEEP IT ON SCREEN
"Company and people data are live from Crustdata. Nobody real is contacted: we play the brand, and email, signatures and
payments are mocks. A real deal takes about two weeks of email; here that time is compressed."

BEFORE ANY RUN
1. python -m app.prewarm                    Warm the cache for known niches.
2. Open each brand you will use once.       So every page answers from the cache.
3. Settings: Watchable pace on (900 ms). Animations Auto or Full.
4. Start from a clean database, then add sample creators. Use labelled samples or teammates' accounts, never real third parties.
5. Rehearse the exact path twice, on the same laptop and network. Keep a recording as the fallback.
6. Have a phone hotspot ready, in case the venue's connection blocks the lookups.

THE PATH (2 minutes)
0:00 One line. "Agencies are paid to find, vet, negotiate and collect. Avuno does the whole job, for any size of creator or brand."
0:10 Creator: show live stats, each with a source and date.
0:25 Brands: live ranking with a reason on each. Find nykaa.com, open Media packs, try a tweak, Apply.
0:40 Second creator applies. A third turns on Auto Deal: packs go out, and skipped brands show why.
0:55 Brand: register as the brand using a team email. Company verified; the sender is not. "It refuses to overclaim, even for us."
1:05 Offers: ranked, with reasons and the rule that decided the order. Choose one.
1:15 Deal: person to approach found on Crustdata, email thread with day labels, live lookups, agreed price inside both limits.
1:35 Re-check runs live before signing. Open the contract, then Sign. Funds held, delivered, released, invoice issued.
1:50 Proof: Deals page, Crash during payment. Resume. One payout, not two.

IF ASKED
Is this real? Data is live. The brand, email, signatures and payments are simulated, and labelled.
Why does it take time on screen? Real lookups take time. A setting also slows steps so viewers can follow, and the page says so.
Where is the AI? Code decides, so the same case gets the same answer. The optional LLM only reads messy text.
Hardest case? Two people share a name, or a contact leaves mid-deal. A name match alone stays weak evidence.
What would you do next? Real delivery checking, sign-in and ownership checks, then real email and payments.

WHAT COULD GO WRONG, AND THE ANSWER
Crustdata slow or down   Pages answer from cache. A failed re-check pauses the deal and says why: show that as a feature.
New niche typed live     Use a pre-warmed niche. A new one fills in over a minute.
Instagram page unread    Use sample creators. The app says "can't verify" rather than guessing.
Deal page stuck          Refresh: the deal resumes from saved state.
Laptop or network dies   Play the recording.
Do not improvise with unrehearsed brands, handles or niches on stage.
