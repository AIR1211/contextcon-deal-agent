IF ASKED
Is this real? Company and people data are live from Crustdata. Brands are played by us; payments and signatures are mocks.
Why is it not instant? Real lookups take time, and the pace setting also slows steps so viewers can follow. The screen says so.
Where is the AI? Code decides, so the same case always gets the same answer. The LLM only reads messy text, and it is optional.
Hardest case? A contact leaves mid-deal, or two people share a name: a name match alone is treated as weak evidence.
