PRODUCT OVERVIEW - ContextCon Deal Agent (working draft, 6 Nov 2026)

ONE LINE
An AI agent that replaces an influencer-marketing agency for small brands and small creators: it takes a
brand's request and carries it through to a signed contract and a paid invoice.

THE SERVICE
Replaces: talent managers and influencer-marketing agencies.
Who pays today: creators pay commission; brands pay retainers or campaign margins.
Who is underserved: small creators and small brands, because small deals are not worth a person's time.
Position: neutral broker. One published rule for both sides, private limits, disclosed fee on closed deals.

END-TO-END FLOW
Creator side: register handle -> read public stats -> rank likely brands -> curated media pack per brand -> optional tweak.
Company side: register domain or Instagram -> verify -> rank creators in the niche -> suggested bundle.
Shared: pitch -> one negotiation engine -> contract -> re-check -> sign -> funds held -> deliver -> approve -> release -> invoice.
A person is asked only on "can't verify", a contact who left, no overlap, or a non-standard clause.

SMALL CREATORS AND COMPANIES (the main focus)
No follower minimum. Creator bundles. Starter media kit. Barter check. Payment protection.
No website required: Instagram, GSTIN or bio code, with stricter terms instead of rejection.
