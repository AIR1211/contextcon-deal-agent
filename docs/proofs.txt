PROOFS - what the maths guarantees, and what it does not
Tests: tests/test_engine.py checks these on thousands of random deals.

NOTATION
r = lowest price acceptable to the creator side, after fees.  c = highest price acceptable to the brand side, after fees.
A deal is possible only if r <= c.

CLAIM 1  The midpoint is the Nash bargaining solution.
The creator gains (p - r) and the brand gains (c - p). Nash's solution maximises g(p) = (p - r)(c - p).
g'(p) = c + r - 2p, which is 0 at p = (r + c)/2, and g''(p) = -2 < 0, so that is the unique maximum. QED
Practical note: prices are whole rupees, so the result may be rounded down by under 1 rupee.

CLAIM 2  Choosing the highest surplus means no listed option beats it for both sides.
Surplus S(t) = c(t) - r(t), and each side gets S(t)/2 at the midpoint. If option u gave both sides more than t*, then
S(u) > S(t*), contradicting the choice of t*. QED
Limit: this covers only listed options and stated limits.

CLAIM 3  Nobody is pushed past their limit, including fees.
Let fc, fb be the fee rates, C the creator cost and V the brand value.
r = ceil(C / (1 - fc)) and c = floor(V / (1 + fb)). With a whole-rupee p and r <= p <= c:
Creator net = p - floor(p fc) = ceil(p (1 - fc)) >= C.
Brand pays = p + ceil(p fb) = ceil(p (1 + fb)) <= V, because V is a whole number. QED

CLAIM 4  In a bundle each creator keeps their limit, and the shares add up.
Each creator receives r_i + a share of (p - sum r_i), with shares summing exactly to the difference. All shares are >= 0,
so p_i >= r_i, and claim 3 applies to each creator. QED

CLAIM 5  The rule is fair and repeatable.
The result depends only on the numbers, not on who is named first or when the request arrived. Tested directly.

WHAT WE CANNOT PROVE
Honest limits: Myerson-Satterthwaite shows no mechanism is always efficient, voluntary, balanced and truth-telling.
We keep the first three and reduce lying with evidence checks, but a side could still gain by misstating its limit.
Likelihood score: weights are our judgement until real outcomes exist. See the backtest in eval/.
Fit score: measures agreement with our rules, not campaign results.
Close estimate and tweak deltas: model estimates, labelled as such in the product.
CPM and views-per-like: assumptions in app/config.py, labelled in the product, to be replaced by real deal data.
