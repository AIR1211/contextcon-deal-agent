KEY FINDING: A NAME MATCH IS NOT IDENTITY
Searching a name at a company matched a different profile with the same name in 4 cases, including one
profile presenting itself as an executive at a company its real namesake left.
Our original test assumed name + company meant the same person. That assumption was wrong, so those rows
fail. We have not changed them.
Design change: name + company is a weak signal only, shown as "name match". Stronger evidence is needed
before a sender counts as verified, such as a work-domain email reply, a profile link checked with Person
Enrich, and a check for several matching profiles.

ALSO LEARNED
Person Enrich is cheap (1 credit in these calls) and showed employers that Search alone did not.
One enriched profile lists both boAt Lifestyle and Imagine Marketing India, a possible source for parent links.
Company Enrich includes monthly_google_ads_budget, but values look low and the unit is unconfirmed.
Job Search: influencer roles found at 4 of 5 brands tested, usable as a budget signal.

show when results became public. It can't prove that the API returned those bytes.
